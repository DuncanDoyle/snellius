**P2 — The diagnosis is directionally right.** FIFO governs SAR *permits*. Permit holders race for tokens, and full-read short-circuits while list and dependency checks run in rounds. Processor sharing is an approximation. Allows expire from SAR start. Commit checks the earliest recorded allow, rebuilds once with fresh request-local state, and carries forward the check count. The cache charges again on an expired miss, including for a previously checked key. An expired allow explains the rebuild. The 50,000 SARs do not establish which misses exhausted the cap. Trace allows, denials, and evictions by attempt. Validity at commit is D-100 item **4** in this checkout. **P2 — Admission risks.** Let cache-only polls bypass admission. On a miss, queue before retaining authorization decisions across the wait, then resolve afresh when admitted. Partly warm polls can still queue. Bound queued requests globally and per viewer, remove canceled waiters promptly, and preserve bounded completion of admitted shared SARs. FIFO protects admitted queue positions from starvation. Keep the 1,024 SAR-waiter cap as a separate backstop and the 1,000-check budget across rebuilds. Denials never trigger commit expiry directly, but their 4–5 minute expiry adds refresh demand and can consume retry budget. Sustained demand above capacity still requires shedding load.
